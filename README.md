## 📌 About the Internship

The internship focused on developing a practical understanding of **Electric Vehicle (EV) technology, modeling, and simulation** using **MATLAB and Simulink**.

The program provided hands-on exposure to EV components and systems including batteries, electric motors, power electronics, vehicle dynamics, powertrain systems, energy consumption, and system-level simulation.

The internship followed a structured learning approach combining theoretical concepts, practical simulation exercises, assignments, and assessments. Simulation was used to understand, analyze, test, and improve EV system performance before real-world implementation.

The internship was conducted under the **AICTE–EduSkills Virtual Internship Program**, supported by **MathWorks**.

## 🏗️ System Architecture

The general EV modeling and simulation workflow followed during the internship consisted of:

```text
Input Parameters
       ↓
EV Component Modeling
       ↓
Battery + Motor + Power Electronics
       ↓
Control & Power Management
       ↓
MATLAB / Simulink Simulation
       ↓
Performance Analysis
       ↓
Optimization
```

The architecture included input parameters such as battery specifications, vehicle speed, motor characteristics, and load conditions. These inputs were used to model EV components, simulate their interaction, analyze parameters such as speed, torque, power, SOC and energy consumption, and identify possible performance improvements.
